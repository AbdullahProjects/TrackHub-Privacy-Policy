# Privacy Policy

**App:** Baby Cry Analyzer
**Last updated:** August 6, 2026

---

## 1. Overview

Baby Cry Analyzer ("the App", "we", "us") is designed to help parents and caregivers understand their baby's cries by analyzing cry audio recordings using an on-device machine learning model.

Your privacy matters to us. **The App is designed to keep your data on your device.** We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information, your baby's information, or your audio recordings with any third party.

---

## 2. Information the App Uses

### 2.1 Audio Recordings (Cry Samples)

- When you use the cry analysis feature, the App records a short audio clip of your baby's cry using your device's microphone.
- The recording is analyzed **locally on your device** using a built-in machine learning model. The audio is **never uploaded to our servers or to any third-party service**.
- After analysis, the recording may be saved **only on your device** so you can review your analysis history. You can delete any recording and the associated history entry at any time, or clear all history and recordings at once.

### 2.2 Baby Profile Information

- You may optionally provide basic profile details about your baby, such as **name, gender, and age**.
- This information is stored **only on your device** (in the App's local storage).
- It is used solely to personalize your experience and label your analysis history. It is never transmitted off your device.

### 2.3 Analysis Results and History

- The App stores analysis results (the detected cry type and confidence level) in your on-device history so you can review past analyses.
- History is limited to the most recent 100 entries, after which the oldest entries (including their audio recordings) are automatically removed from your device.

### 2.4 App Preferences

- Simple preferences such as whether you have completed onboarding, your selected theme, and downloaded content status are stored locally on your device.

---

## 3. Microphone Permission

- The App requests access to your device's microphone **only when you use the cry analysis feature**.
- The audio is processed locally and is not transmitted.
- You can revoke microphone access at any time through your device settings. If you revoke access, the cry analysis feature will not function, but the rest of the App will continue to work.

---

## 4. How We Handle Audio and Media Content

### 4.1 No Cloud Uploads

- The App does **not** upload your recordings, your baby's information, or your analysis history to any server. All cry analysis is performed on-device.

### 4.2 Streaming Media Content

- The App includes a library of pre-recorded content, such as sleep sounds and poems, which is streamed or downloaded from a third-party content delivery service (Cloudinary).
- This content is the same publicly available audio for all users and does not involve any of your personal data.
- Downloaded content is stored on your device only so you can access it offline.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services

The App does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or social media services, and does not integrate with any third-party service that collects personal information.

The only third-party service used is a media content delivery provider (Cloudinary), which is used **solely to deliver pre-recorded audio content** to the App. No personal data or recordings are sent to this service.

---

## 6. Data Security

- All data you create in the App is stored locally on your device.
- We recommend enabling your device's built-in security features (such as a passcode, PIN, or biometric lock) and keeping your operating system up to date to help protect the data stored on your device.
- Because data never leaves your device, there is no transmission of personal information over the network that could be intercepted.

---

## 7. Your Control and Choices

You are always in control of your data:

- **Delete a single history entry** – removes the entry and its associated audio recording from your device.
- **Clear all history** – removes all analysis history and all saved recordings from your device at once.
- **Edit or remove your baby's profile** – you can update or remove profile details at any time.
- **Revoke microphone access** – through your device settings.
- **Uninstall the App** – uninstalling the App removes all locally stored App data from your device.

---

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for use by adults (parents and caregivers) and is not directed to children. The App does not require an account and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Any information about a baby is entered and controlled by the adult using the App and remains on the device.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us (see Section 10) and we will take steps to address the issue.
