# Privacy Policy for VoiceUp - Speak Confidently

**Effective date:** May 17, 2026

Thank you for using VoiceUp - Speak Confidently ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use the App. Please read it carefully. By using the App you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information you provide directly:
- Account information (email, display name) when you register or update your profile
- Profile photo URL if you choose to upload or set a profile image
- Any other information you enter into profile fields, forms, or support requests

### Automatically collected information:
- Usage data and diagnostics to improve the App
- Device identifiers and basic device information
- Advertising identifiers for ad personalization

### Media and device permissions:
- Microphone access for recording practice speech sessions
- Camera and photo library access for profile pictures
- Photos you pick may be uploaded to image hosting services

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- Provide and maintain the App
- Process audio recordings for voice analysis and feedback
- Process image uploads and display your profile photo
- Display personalized advertisements
- Communicate with you (transactional messages or support)
- Detect, investigate, and prevent security issues

---

## 3. Sharing and Disclosure

### Service providers and third-party services:
- **Firebase** - Authentication, Cloud Firestore
- **Cloudinary** - Image uploads and hosting
- **Google AdMob** - Advertising services
- **OpenAI** - AI-powered speech feedback
- **Whisper AI** - Speech-to-text transcription
- Analytics and crash-reporting providers

### Legal:
- We may disclose information when required by law

---

## 4. Audio Recordings and Voice Analysis

The App records your microphone input during practice sessions to analyze your speech. Audio data is processed to provide feedback on confidence, emotion, and speaking skills. Recordings may be transmitted to third-party AI services for analysis. You can delete your recordings and analysis data at any time from the App.

---

## 5. Advertising

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers and advertising data to serve personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings. Rewarded ads are optional and shown only when you choose to watch them.

---

## 6. Image Uploads and Cloudinary

Profile pictures are uploaded to Cloudinary. The App stores the hosted image URL in Firestore. We recommend not uploading sensitive personal information unless you consent.

---

## 7. Data Retention

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy. Audio recordings and analysis data are retained until you delete them or your account. Data deletion requests will be processed according to our retention schedule.

---

## 8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal data. However, no method is 100% secure.

---

## 9. Your Rights and Choices

- **Access and portability** - Request a copy of your data
- **Correction and deletion** - Request changes to your data
- **Permissions** - Manage microphone, camera, and photo access in device settings
- **Ad personalization** - Opt out of personalized ads in device settings

---

## 10. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

---

## 11. Contact

For questions or requests (data export, deletion, corrections), contact: support@voicecoach.com

---

## 12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use indicates acceptance of changes.
